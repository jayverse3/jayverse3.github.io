# Northwest A&F University emblem

- File: `northwest-af-university.svg`
- Source: https://www.nwafu.edu.cn/xxgk/xxbz/index.htm
- Official asset package: https://www.nwafu.edu.cn/docs/2026-03/28de198ca0ab4390adff8a300191c68c.zip
- Converted page 1 of the package's 2020 emblem PDF to SVG with `pdftocairo`; adjusted the SVG viewBox to remove the page margins and removed the rectangular PDF page background to make the area outside the circular emblem transparent. The emblem's internal white field, artwork, and proportions are unchanged.
- Publisher: Northwest A&F University
- Used to identify the university in the personal homepage's Education section; no endorsement implied. Rights remain with the university.

## White variant

- File: `northwest-af-university-white.svg`, adapted locally from the vector artwork in the official PDF; not a separately downloaded university-issued monochrome file.
- Reused the original gold-artwork contour (`clip5`) as an even-odd white path, preserving its exact geometry: the circular borders, school names, year, and central motif. Omitted the white field, green background, gradients, and offset shadow for a transparent monochrome version. No CSS filter is used.
